    Projects

    Alongside my studies I have worked on applied projects with SMEs, municipalities and European partners.

    sustainably.io

    3DxVERSE

    EU co-funded project · since 2025

    3DxVERSE uses digital twins, virtual copies of real places, to support sustainable travel and living communities, from airports to urban neighbourhoods. The project is coordinated by ERTICO (ITS Europe), with pilot cities in Almelo, Aruba, Hamburg and Timișoara.

    Responsible for the communication and dissemination work package, alongside project management and administrative tasks for sustainably.io's part of the project.

    Zuyd University of Applied Sciences

    Second-hand clothing circularity (ARTce)

    KIEM project · student research assistant · supervised by Prof. Dr Nikos Kalogeras, Adriana van Cruysen and Dr Norman Dytianquin; together with my colleague Cynthia Mudamburi

    Global qualitative research on second-hand clothing supply chains in 2024. I presented the findings at ICIB 2024 in Maastricht as “Sustainability in Fashion: Increasing the Inventory Turnover”, while also serving on the conference’s organising committee.

    Vlaai High: PGI-certified regional products and the Limburgse Vlaai

    SME project · student research assistant · supervised by Dr Theo Benos and Prof. Dr Nikos Kalogeras; together with my colleague Cynthia Mudamburi

    Helped SMEs communicate the value of PGI-certified products, focusing on the Limburgse Vlaai, through literature review, stakeholder mapping and primary research (2024 to 2025).

    Smart parking at Qeske Maastricht

    Feasibility study · student research assistant

    Feasibility study of sustainable smart parking for Qeske Maastricht, covering EV charging, solar lighting, green landscaping, dynamic pricing and long-term financial viability (2024 to 2025).
